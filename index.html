The Courses

Tot Hill Farm Golf Club
Mike Strantz, 2000 · Asheboro, NC · ~1 hr from lodging
Par 72 Silver Tees · 6,180 yds Rating 70.6 Slope 135
A wild Mike Strantz design sharing the same bold DNA as Tobacco Road. Dramatic elevation changes, multi-tiered greens protected by rocks and streams, and blind approach shots make this one of the most adventurous rounds in the Carolinas. Recently restored by new ownership and playing better than ever.
🌐 Website 📋 Scorecard
$180 / person · Confirmed & Booked ✓ Nassau
Saturday
Southern Pines Golf Club
Donald Ross, 1906 (Restored by Kyle Franz, 2021) · Southern Pines, NC · <10 min from lodging
Par 71 White Tees · 6,106 yds Rating 70.3 Slope 135
One of the oldest Ross designs in the Sandhills, recently restored to its full glory by Kyle Franz. Short by modern standards but demands precise shot-making with a variety of approaches to each hole. The rugged Sandhills terrain and classic Ross greens make this a must-play — a true gem.
🌐 Website 📋 Scorecard
$245 / person · Booking opens 9/10 Nassau
Sunday
Mid Pines Inn & Golf Club
Donald Ross, 1921 · Southern Pines, NC · 3 min from lodging
Par 72 White Tees · 6,048 yds Rating 70.4 Slope 136
Classic Donald Ross parkland layout perched atop a knoll at the historic Mid Pines Inn. Slight elevation changes, classic bunkering, and fast, sloping greens that will test every part of your game. Sister course to Pine Needles, just across the road. Walkers' paradise.
🌐 Website 📋 Scorecard
$285 / person · Booking opens 9/11 Stableford
Monday
Pine Needles Lodge & Golf Club
Donald Ross, 1927 · Southern Pines, NC · 3 min from lodging
Par 71 White Tees · 6,033 yds Rating 70.0 Slope 133
The crown jewel of the trip and the flagship of the Pines trio. Home to four U.S. Women's Open Championships and ranked among the best courses in America. Wide fairways with classic Ross greens that punish anything short or right. Full resort with locker rooms and showers — perfect final round.
🌐 Website 📋 Scorecard
$315 / person · Booking opens 9/12 Net Stroke
